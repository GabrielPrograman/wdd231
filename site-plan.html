Site Pan: CityPedal Club

Site Name

Name: CityPedal Club

Reason: This name represents a community focused on promoting bicycle commuting as an eco-friendly, safe, and healthy way to get around the city.

Optional domain citypedalclub.org

Site Purpose

The site serves as a meeting hub for urban cyclists. It offers safe route maps for commuting to work or school, basic bike maintenance tips, and a membership signup form to join weekend group rides.

Scenarios

Questions that our target audience might ask:

  • What are the safest bike routes from downtown to the north district?
  • How can I register to participate in the club´s next night ride event?

Color Scheme

The color palette is chosen to reflect and eco-friendly and energetic vibe:

  • Primary Color (#1B4931 - Dark Green): Used for headers, main titles, and the footer to give a professional and environmental feel.
  • Background Color (#F4F9F4 - Soft Pale Green): Used as the main body background to keep the page clean and easy to read.
  • Accent Color (#FF6B6B - Coral/Vibrant Red): Used for links, important highlights, and call-to-action buttons.

Typography

Fonts selected for the website:

  • Heading Font:'Poppins' sans-serif. Used for all headings (h1, h2) to provide a modern, structural look.
  • Body Font: 'Open Sans' sans-serif. Used for paragraphs and list to ensure great legibility.
