Urban Bike Maintenance

Perform these basic pre-ride checks to ensure safety on your daily commute.

1. Air Pressure Inspection

Ensure your tires are properly inflated to the PSI range stamped on the tire sidewall. Correct air pressure prevents pinch flats and reduces rolling resistance.

2. Brake System Check

Inspect brake pads for wear and test both front and rear levers squeeze firmly without touching the handlebar grips.

3. Chain Cleanliness & Drivetrain Lube

Wipe away accumulated road grime with a rag and apply bicycle-specific chain lubricant every few weeks to keep gear shifts crisp and prevent rust.
